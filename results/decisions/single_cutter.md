# Single-cutter keep-or-change decisions on 13 real held-out cutters

Generated by `scripts/07_single_cutter_decisions.py`. Each of the 13 usable Rzeszów cutters is held out in turn. Every policy is tuned on the other 12 cutters (using failure curves from models that saw neither that training cutter nor the held-out one), then replays the held-out cutter cycle by cycle. The real failure cycle is known, so outcomes are exact; **only the costs are assumed** (`research/next-phase/costs.md`). Scores pool the 13 cutters: total cost ÷ total good cycles. Brackets are 95% intervals from resampling the 13 cutters (paired with the fixed interval).

Prediction sources: `weibull_settings_age`, `lgbm_hazard`, `history_physics`, `history_settings_age`. `weibull_settings_age` and `lgbm_hazard` are the yardsticks of step 06 (`results/predictions/summary.csv` has their accuracy).

Policies: **never** = run to failure; **fixed** = fixed interval; **fixed_group** = fixed interval per width; **rul_q10** = cautious remaining life (q10); **hazard** = risk threshold; **one_step** = one-step cost rule; **dp** = exact DP (g from costs); **dp_tuned** = exact DP (g tuned); **oracle** = perfect knowledge.

## Reading these numbers

- 13 cutters is the sample size; a policy's breakage count moves by whole cutters. Intervals are wide.
- The DP is optimal only if the curve it is given is right. The yardstick curves are not calibrated, so the DP can lose to a tuned threshold that absorbs the miscalibration in its tuned value.
- `dp` trusts the curve completely: g comes from Dinkelbach's iteration on the training replays. When the curves miss sudden failures, those breakages push g up, and a high g makes keeping look better, so `dp` can settle on a poor fixed point when breakages are expensive. `dp_tuned` grid-searches g on the training cutters, the same tuning effort the thresholds get. A one-step rule with a tuned g is exactly the risk threshold (`hazard`).
- The fixed interval uses no predictions; DP and the rules use the curves. The oracle is unreachable.
- Wasted cycles count good cycles left on cutters changed early; a breakage wastes none but costs C_f.
